## About this site

This site is maintained by **Izar Tarandach** and **Matthew J. Coles**, co-authors of
[Threat Modeling: A Practical Guide for Development Teams](https://www.amazon.com/Threat-Modeling-Identification-Avoidance-Secure/dp/1492056553/) (O'Reilly, 2020).

It exists as a home for the book, and as an ongoing place to write about threat
modeling: practice, tools, methodology, and how the discipline is changing as
AI and LLMs get pulled into development workflows.

## The authors
